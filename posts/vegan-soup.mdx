# Vegan Soup

This is my go-to pressure cooker soup recipe.

- 1 tbsp veggie boullion
- 1-2 potatoes
- 1/4 onion
- 1 jalapeño pepper (w/o seeds)
- 4 mushrooms
- 4 brussel sprouts
- 1 tbsp cilantro
- 2 tbsp wild rice
- 2 tbsp raw pinto beans
- 2 tbsp Beyond Steak
- 3qt water

Dice everything up and pressure cook for 13 minutes, serves 4.
