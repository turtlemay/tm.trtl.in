# Thoughts on American Psycho (2000)
2025-10-10T04:53:36-04:00

Probably my most-watched film. It's presented as a thriller but is actually a brilliant absurdist dark comedy.

I will never not giggle like a schoolgirl at the crossword scene where Bateman is reclined in his office with sunglasses on, nonchalantly filling the page with chaotic variations of the words "meat" and "bones" and proximately asking his assistant on a date to the restaurant he can't get into.
