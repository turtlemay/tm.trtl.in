# Purpose of this site
2025-10-18T01:35:56-04:00

I am creating this site in support of the "Small Web" movement. Like the indie arts, the idea might be summed up as an advocacy for certain creative freedoms and the usage of simple, open and accessible tools over large, corporate-controlled platforms. And perhaps more importantly, it is counter to the technical bloat, degeneracy, and mass commercialization of the social media industry and commoditization of our attention.

Web crawlers and AI are not welome here.

Beyond the 80s and 90s nostalgia, I have gone the rather brutalist approach of a static, text-only format which—in keeping with the idea of a low-bandwidth, information-first Internet—is accessible directly from the terminal as well as mainstream browsers of any generation. (It was originally meant to be Gopher hole before I ruled out alt protocols for practical reasons.) 

While I recommend a terminal browser such as `lynx` (available on all platforms), those that prefer a variable-width font or narrator can easily use their preferred tool to read these pages. Any external links I include on the site should be consumable in the same manner.

All content is also available via [rss feed](/rss.xml) which you can consume from a terminal using `newsboat` or your preferred feed reader.

My more robust content that doesn't fit here can be found at `www.turtlemay.us`.
